# 🔒 Privacy Policy — Ranger DLP

*Last updated: October 15, 2026*

Ranger DLP ("the extension") is designed to **protect** your data, not to collect it. This policy explains, in plain language, how the extension handles information.

## 1. Summary

**Ranger DLP does not collect, transmit, sell or share any data. Ever.**

All processing happens locally, inside your browser, on your own device.

## 2. What the extension processes

| Data | How it is used | Leaves your device? |
|------|----------------|:-------------------:|
| Text you paste or drag & drop | Inspected in memory against the detection rules, then masked or blocked if needed | ❌ No |
| Your settings (enabled rules, protection mode) | Saved with standard browser storage APIs so your preferences persist | ❌ No |

Inspected text is **not stored, logged or cached** after the paste or drop action completes.

## 3. What the extension does NOT do

- ❌ Does not collect personal data
- ❌ Does not read or record your browsing history
- ❌ Does not record keystrokes
- ❌ Does not send clipboard contents anywhere
- ❌ Does not use analytics, tracking or telemetry
- ❌ Does not contact any external server
- ❌ Does not sell or share data with third parties

## 4. Permissions

The extension requests only the browser permissions it needs to inspect paste and drop events on web pages and to save your settings locally. These permissions are never used to collect or transmit data.

## 5. Data retention and deletion

Since no data is collected, nothing is retained. Your local settings can be removed at any time by resetting the extension or uninstalling it.

## 6. Children's privacy

The extension does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

## 7. Changes to this policy

Any changes to this policy will be published in this repository with an updated date.

## 8. Contact

For questions about this policy, please open an issue in this repository.
